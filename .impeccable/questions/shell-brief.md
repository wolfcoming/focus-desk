# App shell / Today

## Scope and mode
Whole Little Fun desktop UI redesign, Operate mode. Primary surface: app shell + Today (今日三件). Related: Todos, Projects, Progress, Weekly, Settings, Reminder.

## Audience and job
Solo knowledge worker at a personal desk, keeping a few commitments clear. Job: see today’s focus, check light todos and dailies, open projects without a crowded dashboard.

## Direction
透光看片台 (photographer’s light table). Seed key 7f83fdce; form #1 on the grounded list (user chose IMPECCABLE’S PICK over the roll).

## Memorable moment
今日三件 as three luminous plates on a cool work surface; the lit plate is what you’re doing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Little Fun is a cool light table for today’s work—three illuminated plates hold focus, never a cream journal or metric dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Mist-blue shell (#D7E4EC rail), luminous cool ground (#F4F8FB), graphite ink (#2A3640), slate accent (#5B7C8A), amber only for due/attention (#B45309). Soft frosted-plane density; thin metal-edge dividers; workhorse geometric UI type with CJK system fallback.

STORY: Visitor opens to a clear desk: rail for place, main surface for today’s three plates then dailies/todos; they act on a plate or a checkbox without hunting chrome.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left mist-blue rail (brand, nav, capacity note). Main luminous field: heading 今天, then a row of three light-table plates for 今日三件 (empty plates show faint frosted slots); below, 今天的日常 then light todos. Primary action sits on each plate (open / mark) and the add/suggest controls under the plate row.

FORM: Photographer’s light table; position 1 of 7 grounded candidates; seed key 7f83fdce.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
